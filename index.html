01 — ABOUT

From teaching technology to building it.

I am a passionate Laravel developer who enjoys turning ideas into practical web applications. My journey into software development has been shaped by education, teaching, career exploration and continuous hands-on learning.

I completed my M.Com from Calicut University and PGDCA from IHRD. During my PGDCA studies, I developed an employment exchange project using .NET.

I then worked as a computer teacher at Silver Stone School, Calicut for four years. Teaching strengthened my communication skills and my ability to explain technical concepts clearly.

After a career break focused on competitive examination preparation, I returned to software development with a strong focus on practical programming.

In January 2025, I started a software development internship and completed it in May 2025. Since then, I have continued developing Laravel projects and expanding my knowledge of APIs, React and modern web development.
